Ketone Test Strip Pad Highlight

  • What are Ketones?
    Ketones are chemical compounds produced when your body breaks down fat for energy instead of carbohydrates. This process happens when glucose (sugar) is unavailable or cannot be used efficiently by your cells.

  • Why Test for Ketones?
    Testing for urinary ketones helps monitor fat metabolism, low-carbohydrate or ketogenic diets, prolonged fasting, and intense exercise. For individuals with diabetes, tracking elevated ketones is critical to prevent Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA).

  • Reading the Results (mmol/L):
    • Negative (NEG): Normal / No ketones detected.
    • 1.5 - 3.0: Low to Moderate (Mild ketosis/fasting state).
    • 7.5 - ≥15: High to Very High (Potential DKA risk; consult a healthcare professional).
