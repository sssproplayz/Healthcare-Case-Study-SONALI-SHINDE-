pH Test Strip Pad Highlight

  • What is Urine pH?
    Urine pH measures how acidic or alkaline (basic) your urine is on a scale from 1 to 14. A neutral level sits at 7, while values below 7 indicate acidity and values above 7 indicate alkalinity.

  • Why Test for pH?
    Monitoring urine pH helps evaluate body acid-base balance, diet impact, and risk for certain types of kidney stones. It can also point to urinary tract infections (UTIs) or kidney tubular disorders.

  • Reading the Results (pH Scale):
    • 5.0 - 6.0: Acidic (Common with high-protein diets, keto diets, or dehydration).
    • 6.0 - 7.0: Typical/Neutral range for average balanced diets.
    • 8.0 - 9.0: Alkaline (Common with vegetarian/plant-based diets or certain bacterial UTIs).
