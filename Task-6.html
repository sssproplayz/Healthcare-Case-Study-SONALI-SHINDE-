  • Urine test strips detect various chemicals and substances in your urine, including protein, glucose, and blood. They use small color-changing pads to screen for signs of kidney disease, diabetes, and urinary tract infections in about one to two minutes.

  • A standard urine test (urinalysis) checks your urine's visual look, chemical makeup, and microscopic content. It can show signs of urinary tract infections (UTIs), kidney disease, diabetes, liver problems, and dehydration by detecting abnormal levels of blood, protein, sugar, bacteria, and white blood cells.
