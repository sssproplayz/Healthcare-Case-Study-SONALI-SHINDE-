About the System

The Urine Test Strip Reader is designed to make urine analysis more objective, automated, and reliable at the point of care. Conventional test strips often require visual comparison with a printed colour chart, which can lead to differences in interpretation. This system aims to use controlled illumination, Computer Vision, and AI-based analysis to process all 10 reagent pads and generate continuous numerical results.



Objectives

The key objectives of the system are:

  • To reduce errors caused by ambient lighting and subjective visual interpretation.
  • To automatically detect and analyze all 10 reagent pads on a urine test strip.
  • To extract colour information and map it to continuous analyte concentrations using non-linear regression or deep learning.
  • To automate the workflow from strip placement to digital report generation.
  • To securely link results with a patient ID and timestamp for monitoring and record management.


Working Principle

The system operates as a standalone point-of-care diagnostic unit powered by a Raspberry Pi. The urine test strip is placed inside an opaque black-box enclosure containing a strip holder, calibrated LED illumination, and a camera module.

First, the biochemical reaction is allowed to develop for a timed 120-second period. The camera then captures the strip under uniform and controlled lighting conditions. An OpenCV-based Computer Vision pipeline isolates each reagent pad and calculates its average RGB values.

These colour values are then passed to a trained non-linear regression model, which predicts numerical concentrations for the analytes. The final results are compiled into a structured report and securely logged for clinical review.



Benefits

The system offers several potential benefits for healthcare and point-of-care testing:

  • Objective Analysis: Reduces dependence on subjective visual comparison of colours.
  • Controlled Environment: The black-box enclosure and calibrated LED lighting help minimize environmental variation.
  • Automation: The process automatically performs timed image capture, pad analysis, and result generation.
  • Continuous Results: The AI model is designed to provide numerical concentrations rather than only categorical ranges.
  • Standalone Operation: The complete pipeline runs on the device without requiring a smartphone or external laptop.
  • Secure Data Management: Results can be linked to patient information and securely transmitted to a hospital-compatible database.
  • Clinical Decision Support: Abnormal values can be highlighted for clinician or pathologist review.

Healthcare Note: The system is intended to function as a clinical decision-support tool. Flagged or abnormal results should be reviewed by a qualified clinician or pathologist before clinical action is taken.
