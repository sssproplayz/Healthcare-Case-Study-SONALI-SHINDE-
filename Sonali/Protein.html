Protein Test Strip Pad Highlight

  • What is Urine Protein?
    Protein (primarily albumin) is essential for overall body function. Healthy kidneys filter waste products from the blood while keeping vital proteins in the bloodstream, leaving only trace amounts in urine.

  • Why Test for Protein?
    Detecting protein in urine (proteinuria) helps evaluate kidney performance and early signs of kidney damage. Temporary spikes can also be caused by intense exercise, dehydration, high fever, or stress.

  • Reading the Results (g/L):
    • Negative (NEG): Normal / No significant protein detected.
    • 0.3 - 1.0: Trace to Moderate protein concentration.
    • 3.0 - ≥10: High concentration (Indicates potential kidney filter dysfunction; consult a healthcare professional).
