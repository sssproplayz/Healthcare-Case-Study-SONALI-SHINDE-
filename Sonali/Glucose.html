Glucose Test Strip Pad Highlight

  • What is Urine Glucose?
    Glucose is a simple sugar that serves as your body's main energy source. Under normal conditions, the kidneys reabsorb virtually all glucose back into the bloodstream, leaving little to none in the urine.

  • Why Test for Glucose?
    Testing for glucose in urine is primarily used to screen for or monitor diabetes mellitus, high blood sugar (hyperglycemia), or kidney threshold alterations, such as renal glycosuria during pregnancy.

  • Reading the Results (mg/dL):
    • Negative (NEG): Normal / No detectable glucose in urine.
    • 100 - 300: Mild to Moderate glucose spilling (possible high blood sugar).
    • 1000 - 3000: High to Very High (Indicates significantly elevated blood sugar; consult a healthcare provider).
